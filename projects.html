Ballpark Shade

I started this after looking for Padres tickets on a hot day in San Diego and wanting to know which seats at Petco Park would stay in the shade for most of the game.

The app combines the sun’s position for a selected date and time with simplified 3D stadium geometry. You can explore Petco Park, Dodger Stadium, and Oracle Park, choose a section, and watch the shadows move through a game.
