How to read the signals

Compass arrows show where swell and wind are traveling; “from” labels show their source direction. Wind cues assume a roughly west-facing beach near PB / La Jolla, not the whole county.

Swell cues are a quick heuristic: under 1.5 ft is small; under 10 seconds is short-period; 10–13 seconds is mid-period; 13+ seconds is longer-period. Longer periods can mean more powerful sets, not necessarily better shape. Large swell (6+ ft) gets a separate caution color.

Spot bars compare nearshore modeled height, not breaking-wave faces. “More size” and “Less size” compare fresh readings at similar times, not surf quality. Check the cams for shape, crowding, and local wind. Offshore swell and nearshore peak period use different model measures.
